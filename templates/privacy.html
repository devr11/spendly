Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

At Spendly, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal and financial information when you use our expense tracking service. By using Spendly, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Spendly, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and password (stored securely in hashed form).
  • Expense Data: Transaction amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and any notes you add to your expenses.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the Service, including pages visited, features used, and session duration.
  • Device Information: Your IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers for analytics and security purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the expense tracking service.
  • Personalise your experience and display your spending summaries.
  • Improve the Service based on usage patterns and feedback.
  • Communicate with you about updates, security alerts, or support requests.
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption. We use HTTPS to protect data in transit and employ security measures such as password hashing, access controls, and regular security audits. While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and expense data for as long as your account remains active. If you choose to delete your account, we will remove your personal information and expense records within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, and email delivery. These providers have access only to the data necessary to perform their functions and are contractually obligated to protect your information. We do not share your expense data with third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and download your data at any time.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information in your account.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of non-essential communications.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Spendly uses cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session, remember your preferences, and analyse how the Service is used. You can control cookie settings through your browser, but disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Service.

9. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will notify you via email or through the Service. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
